    What this does and does not do

    Privacy. The domain you type is sent to Cloudflare's public DNS-over-HTTPS service, and to Google's if Cloudflare does not answer. Nothing is sent to me and nothing is stored.

    Limits. It reads DNS records. It does not check blacklists or inbox placement, and it cannot test that a real message passes. A DKIM selector cannot be discovered from a domain, so it tries the common ones; type yours if it is not found. A clean result does not guarantee inbox placement.

    Why these checks. Gmail and Yahoo require SPF, DKIM and DMARC from anyone who sends in bulk.

    Part of a portfolio of small builds. The checking logic is unit-tested against fake DNS answers, and this page is generated from that tested code. Source and tests.
